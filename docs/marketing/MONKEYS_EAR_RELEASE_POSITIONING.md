# Monkey's Ear release positioning

## Product identity

**Monkey's Ear is a suite/ecosystem of audio modules and plugins.** Individual modules can stand alone, and the longer-term convergence target is a much larger integrated VSTi built from the suite.

That future integrated VSTi is **not** the current commercial release claim.

## Current release frontier — 2026-09-16

The near-term release candidate is the **Voice/Vocal plugin**. It is the only Monkey's Ear module currently close enough to productization to justify a revenue-release gate.

Other Monkey's Ear modules and the integrated VSTi remain development work. Their existence, buildability, tests, or shared architecture must not be turned into a claim that the full suite is ready for customers.

## Marketing law

Public marketing should distinguish three layers:

1. **Monkey's Ear** — the suite/ecosystem and long-term product family.
2. **Voice/Vocal** — the current near-release standalone plugin and first commercialization candidate.
3. **Integrated VSTi** — the eventual large converged instrument, explicitly future-facing until the modules are mature enough to justify that claim.

For the first revenue experiment, calls to action should point to Voice/Vocal testing, demonstrations, early access, and user feedback—not to a supposedly finished Monkey's Ear instrument.
