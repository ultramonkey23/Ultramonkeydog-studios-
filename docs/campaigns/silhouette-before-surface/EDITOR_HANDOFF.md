# Editor-copy publication handoff

Campaign: convert a finished owned-site article into a frictionless, public reviewer package without duplicating the private marketing owner or inventing publication permission.

Audience: editors and publishers considering practical game-design writing.

Public outputs:

- `/dispatches/silhouette-before-surface-editor-copy`
- `/media/silhouette-before-surface-editor-copy.txt`
- canonical source remains `/dispatches/silhouette-before-surface`

The page contains the full article, headline, byline, word count, topic, summary, asset decision, reuse boundary, direct contact action and downloadable plain-text edition. The download is generated from `article.md` during the discovery build so the public copy cannot silently drift from the canonical article.

Claim boundaries:

- No external outlet submission or acceptance is claimed.
- No payment, placement, syndication right or editor interest is claimed.
- No image package is required; the edition is intentionally text-first.
- The illustrative shield creature is not a shipped feature.
- No unreleased-game completion, accessibility, player-study or audio-quality claim is made.

Visitor action: assess the full copy, download it if useful, then contact Cody about publication or edits.

Learning signal: a specific editorial inquiry mentioning the article. Page views and downloads alone do not prove consideration or intent.

Rollback: revert the publication commit through a new non-force commit. Do not rewrite canonical history.
